# Drift — Product Vision

**Date:** 2025-02-12

Drift is a local-first field-notes app for the Verida Atoll Marine Survey.

Core promises:
1. A diver can always create or edit a note, even with no network at all.
2. Notes sync automatically when connectivity returns; the diver never presses
   a "sync" button.
3. Every observation is attributable to a diver and a sector.

The app catalogues sightings of fictional species endemic to Verida Atoll, such
as the Verida bubblefish and the kelp-glider eel. These species are invented for
survey training purposes and do not exist outside this project.
