# Drift — Accessibility Notes

**Date:** 2025-03-08

The tablet UI must be usable in bright sunlight and on wet screens:

- All text meets a minimum 18pt equivalent at arm's length.
- Colour is never the sole carrier of information; every status has a text
  label and an icon.
- Touch targets are at least 44x44 points.
- The sector selector has four large buttons (north, east, south, west), each
  with a distinct icon, not just colour.

Theo owns these accessibility rules and reviews every UI change against them.
