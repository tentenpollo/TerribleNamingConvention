# Drift — Team Charter

**Project:** Drift — a local-first field-notes application for the Verida Atoll Marine Survey.
**Team:** Drift core team
**Date:** 2025-02-10

## Purpose
Drift lets marine biologists of the Verida Atoll Marine Survey record field
observations on small boats with no reliable network, then sync later. The team
building Drift is small: three engineers and one biologist liaison.

## Operating constraints
- Field devices are ruggedized tablets with intermittent connectivity.
- The Verida Atoll reef is divided into four fictional survey sectors: North,
  East, South, and West.
- The app must work fully offline and never block a diver from logging a note.
