# Drift — Recurring Themes

**Date:** 2025-03-22

Themes that come up repeatedly in Drift team discussions:

- **Offline reliability** — every feature is judged against "does it work with
  no network?"
- **Data durability** — field data must survive power loss and device failure.
- **Diver ergonomics** — the UI must be usable with cold, wet hands on a small
  tablet.
- **Sector attribution** — every observation must carry its survey sector.
- **Sync predictability** — divers should be able to reason about when their
  notes will appear on other devices.

These themes are the lens for prioritising new work.
