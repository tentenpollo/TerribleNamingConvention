# Drift — Observation Workflow

**Date:** 2025-02-26

A diver's workflow in Drift:

1. Open the app on the tablet (works offline).
2. Select the current survey sector (north, east, south, or west).
3. Create a new observation with species, count, depth, and notes.
4. Save locally. The note is immediately readable on that tablet.
5. Sync happens automatically in the background when the network is available.

Dr. Wynne reviews flagged conflicts on Mondays via radio from the atoll. Maren
and Theo pair-review the sync engine on Wednesdays.
